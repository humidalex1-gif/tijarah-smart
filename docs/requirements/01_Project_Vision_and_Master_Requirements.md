# Smart Inventory & Financial System
## File 01 — Project Vision & Master Requirements

**Suggested project name:** Tijarah Smart  
**Alternative names:** StockFlow AI, MerchantOS, Hisab Smart, TradePilot  
**Document version:** 1.0  
**Project type:** Cloud-based Inventory, Sales, Expense, Profitability, and Financial Management System  
**Primary languages:** Arabic and English  
**Target users:** Small and medium merchants, e-commerce businesses, wholesalers, retailers, warehouses, and multi-branch businesses

---

# 1. Project Vision

Build a smart cloud-based business management platform that combines:

- Inventory tracking
- Product costing
- Purchases and suppliers
- Sales and customers
- Expenses
- Logistics costs
- Advertising costs
- Profit and loss calculations
- Cash flow
- Multi-warehouse management
- E-commerce integrations
- Smart reports
- AI business assistant

The system must help merchants understand:

- How many products they own
- Where every product is stored
- What entered and left the warehouse
- The real cost of every product
- Total sales and expenses
- Gross profit and net profit
- Which products are profitable
- Which products are losing money
- How much money is tied up in inventory
- When to reorder products
- Which products are slow-moving
- Which advertisements generate profit
- The current financial health of the business

The platform should not be only an inventory tracker and should not be only an accounting tool. It should be a complete smart operating system for merchants.

---

# 2. Main Business Problem

Many merchants currently use separate tools for:

- Inventory
- Excel sheets
- Sales
- Expenses
- Advertising reports
- Logistics costs
- Supplier payments
- Customer invoices
- Profit calculations

This creates several problems:

- Incorrect inventory quantities
- Lost or duplicated data
- Difficulty calculating real product cost
- Difficulty knowing the true net profit
- Advertising costs not linked to product sales
- Logistics and customs costs not included correctly
- No clear view of cash flow
- No warning when products are running out
- No easy way to track damaged, returned, or lost products
- No central dashboard for the whole business

The system will solve these problems by placing all business information in one connected platform.

---

# 3. Core Project Goals

The system must:

1. Track all incoming and outgoing inventory.
2. Calculate the real landed cost of each product.
3. Calculate gross profit and net profit automatically.
4. Record all business income and expenses.
5. Connect inventory with sales and finance.
6. Support multiple warehouses and branches.
7. Support barcode and QR code scanning.
8. Support manual sales and online store sales.
9. Integrate with e-commerce platforms such as Salla.
10. Provide intelligent alerts and recommendations.
11. Provide Arabic and English interfaces.
12. Be easy for non-technical merchants.
13. Work on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
14. Store information securely in the cloud.
15. provide a complete audit trail for every important action.

# 6. Real Product Cost Calculation

The system must calculate the real landed cost of every product.

The real product cost may include:

- Supplier purchase price
- International shipping
- Local shipping
- Customs fees
- Import duties
- Insurance
- Packaging
- Warehouse receiving fees
- Payment gateway fees
- Currency conversion fees
- Agent commission
- Inspection fees
- Port fees
- Handling fees
- Storage fees
- Other costs

The system should distribute shared costs across products using:

- Quantity
- Weight
- Volume
- Product value
- Manual percentage
- Equal distribution

Example formula:

**Landed Cost Per Unit = Product Purchase Cost + Allocated Logistics Cost + Allocated Customs Cost + Allocated Other Costs**

The user should be able to select the allocation method for every shipment or purchase order.

# 11. Profitability Calculations

The system must calculate profitability at multiple levels:

- Profit per sale
- Profit per product
- Profit per product variant
- Profit per order
- Profit per customer
- Profit per sales channel
- Profit per warehouse
- Profit per branch
- Profit per campaign
- Profit per month
- Profit per year
- Profit for the whole business

Main calculations:

**Revenue = Product Sales + Shipping Charged + Other Income**

**Gross Profit = Revenue - Cost of Goods Sold**

**Net Profit = Revenue - Product Cost - Logistics - Advertising - Platform Fees - Payment Fees - Operating Expenses - Refunds - Discounts - Taxes and Other Costs**

**Profit Margin = Net Profit ÷ Revenue × 100**

The system must clearly distinguish between:

- Revenue
- Gross profit
- Operating profit
- Net profit
- Cash received
- Outstanding payments

# 25. Final Project Instruction

Build this system as a professional cloud SaaS platform for merchants.

Do not build disconnected pages or placeholder dashboards.

All modules must share one consistent database and calculations.

Every inventory movement must affect inventory.

Every completed sale must affect:

- Inventory
- Revenue
- Customer balance
- Cash or bank balance
- Product cost
- Gross profit
- Net profit

Every purchase receipt must affect:

- Inventory quantity
- Product cost
- Supplier balance
- Inventory value

Every expense must affect:

- Financial reports
- Cash or bank balance
- Net profit

The system must always make calculations transparent. Users should be able to click any total and see exactly how it was calculated.

The experience should be simple enough for a new merchant but powerful enough for a growing company.
